The pilots behind our Sunday fleets. Explore attendance, arrests, final blows, and pod kills for each year or all time.

How the rankings work
  • Attendance: one credit per fleet where a pilot appears as an attacker on a recorded killmail. Pilots who attend without appearing on a killmail cannot be counted.
  • Arrests: one credit per killmail where the pilot appears as an attacker, including capsule kills. Multiple appearances on the same killmail count once.
  • Final blows: kills where the pilot delivered the final blow, including capsule kills.
  • Pod kills: capsule kills where the pilot appears as an attacker, whether or not they delivered the final blow.

Includes all attacking pilots on WHPD alliance W-space killmails during completed Sunday fleets from 1 to 3 PM Eastern, starting in July 2020. Only fleets with recorded ISK value are included, matching the weekly reports. Years follow the Eastern fleet date. Equal scores share a rank; pilots with no score are omitted from that ranking.
